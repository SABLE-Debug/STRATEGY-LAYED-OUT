SPLIT
03 · THE SPLIT

What AI does.
What only you can.

AI does the volume. You do the judgment. The 80/20 split works: AI produces 80% of the typing, you produce 20% — but your 20% is what makes the other 80% land. Without the human signal, AI is just remixing nothing.

THE STRATEGIST

AI · Volume Work

  • Drafting 5 X posts from your Sunday Loom
  • Rewriting 3 LinkedIn long-form posts/week from the same Loom
  • Drafting weekly newsletter from the Loom transcript
  • Researching 50 prospects/week — bios, recent posts, pain points
  • Drafting 25 cold X DMs + 15 LinkedIn DMs in your voice
  • Suggesting which engagers to DM based on post interaction
  • Drafting reply messages for warm conversations
  • Scheduling all content via Buffer
  • Logging metrics, killing low-performers
  • Generating client deliverables once contracts close
AKHANYA

Human · Judgment Work

  • Be the face — proof AKHARA isn't another AI-generated agency
  • Record one 20-min Loom every Sunday — the raw material everything compounds from
  • Approve every output before send (Month 1), spot-check 25% (Month 2+)
  • The first 30 seconds of every discovery call — read the room, adjust
  • The actual close — "What's stopping us from starting Monday?" + four seconds of silence
  • Strategic decisions — pricing, tier expansion, which prospects to fire
  • Reading subtext on inbound replies — AI can't tell tire-kicker from buyer
  • Picking which 5 clients to take when 10 qualify
  • Showing up on calls — discovery, kickoff, QBR
  • Being reachable — the trust signal that competitors can't fake

Your real job = Director of Brand + Closer. The Strategist is your assistant. You're the principal. AI handles 80% of the typing. You handle 100% of the decisions, recordings, and closes.

SIX
07 · ACCELERATION

Six moves.
To beat the clock.

If you want $40–80K in six months, these are the six adjustments to the master file plan. Each one compresses time without compromising quality. Execute all six.

Compress Phase A from "next weekend" to "this Friday"

Every day of setup delay = one fewer day of compounding distribution. Domain bought Monday. DNS live Tuesday. First social posts Wednesday. First cold DMs Thursday. No Saturday/Sunday for setup. Those become content recording days.

Start outbound Day 1, not Day 8

Master file says start outbound Week 2. Skip that. Send 5 X DMs/day starting the day akhara.co resolves. The Closer can be live by Day 3, so it drafts while you approve. First replies hit by Week 1 Day 5. First close moves from Week 6 to Week 3.

Double the prospect pool, run two niches in parallel

Instead of 5 med spa + 5 real estate, identify 20 prospects per niche in Week 1 (40 total, 10/day touched). At 25% reply rate that's 10 replies Week 1, 3–4 demos Week 2, first close Week 3–4.

Add personalized founder email (not list-bought)

10 emails/day, hand-personalized from public sources (X bio, LinkedIn, company About page). This isn't cold email at volume — this is founder-to-founder outreach via email. Reply rate 15–20%. No domain warmup needed because volume = 10/day. Doesn't violate the no-Smartlead rule.

Use the playbook itself as your lead magnet

Your playbook is already public on akhara.co. That's a $5K consulting deliverable given away free. Every X post, LinkedIn post, DM, newsletter footer → "Full system at akhara.co." Prospects who scroll the whole thing self-qualify. This is the unfair advantage — most agencies show a landing page. You show the entire operation. Prospects who DM after reading have already sold themselves.

Compress Manual Mode test from 7 days to 3

Master file says 7-day Manual Mode test before Semi-Auto. If you're approving every output anyway and the test matrix passes at 95%+ by Day 3, graduate. Save 4 days. Compounds across all 4 employees.

RAMP
08 · THE QUESTION

Do we ramp
distribution harder?

Honest answer: not yet. The three channels in Section 02 are already maxed for what AKHARA can absorb in Months 1–3. Adding a fourth channel now spreads attention thin and dilutes the brand signal. Discipline beats volume.

What adds without diluting:

  • Month 1–3: Top 3 channels (X · LinkedIn · Newsletter) + the personalized founder-email move. Four total streams.
  • Month 4: Add cold email at volume (50–75/day, warmed domain, case-study backed). Channel #5.
  • Month 6: Add referral engine (current clients refer for $1K cash or 1 month at 50% off). Channel #6.
  • Month 9: Add YouTube long-form (1 video/month, repurposed from newsletter teardowns). Channel #7.
  • Month 12+: Add cold calls — Sovereign tier only, founder-to-founder. Channel #8.

The principle: Each channel takes ~3 months to mature. Stacking channels too fast = none of them get to depth. Master 3 channels in 3 months. Add 1 channel per 3 months after.

The one thing that does scale immediately: Once a single channel is producing leads, double down on it — more posts, more DMs, more issues. Don't chase a new channel until the current one plateaus.
